About me

Hi, I'm Minh Anh, though most people know me as Emery.

I use data and AI to help businesses grow, and I research how AI quietly shapes the way we behave and make decisions. While I'm optimistic about what it can do for our work, I'm also curious about the future we're heading into, where humans and AI live side by side, and about what we should do to make that go well.

I value meaningful human connections and people’s well-being. Outside of work, I bring that same interest in people to my travels: I love immersing myself in different cultures around the world.
