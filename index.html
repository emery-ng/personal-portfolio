How much should we order tomorrow?

Every food business faces this: order too much and food is wasted, too little and you run out. A foodservice client was deciding on instinct. I tested whether the weather explained their sales, and at one location it did: rain and humidity, not temperature, predicted the slow days. Any business with uncertain demand can use the same approach.

What does one missing item cost your business?

For a caterer, it's a trip back in the truck or a ruined event. A foodservice client built its packing lists by hand from invoices, which invites mistakes. I built a tool that writes the list from the invoice and flags anything it isn't sure of. Any business that fills orders from documents can use the same idea.
