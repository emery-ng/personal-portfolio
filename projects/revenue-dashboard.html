Profitability Analysis in Power BI

Business intelligence · Course project · Food products

21,750 order lines July 2019 – August 2024
24% net margin 12.96M gross, 3.12M net
5 tables, 4 links Cleaned in Power Query, modeled in Power BI

The question

Where does revenue come from, and where do costs eat into it? Using order data from a food products business, I built a dashboard that shows revenue, cost, and net revenue by region, product, and supplier.

What I built

  • Imported and cleaned the data: Loaded five tables from a spreadsheet. The Suppliers sheet didn't import cleanly, so I set its column types and promoted its header row in Power Query before loading.
  • Modeled it: Linked orders to customers and products, products to suppliers, and suppliers to countries.
  • Calculated the measures: Gross revenue, cost, net revenue, and net margin %.
  • Built three linked views and combined them into one dashboard: By region with drill-down to country and supplier, by product, and by supplier and country.

What it shows

  • The Americas bring in about half of gross revenue (6.67M of 12.96M). Europe earns a lower margin than the other two regions, at 22% versus about 25%.
  • Hot Pepper Sauce is one of the top-selling products but earns only 11%, well below the typical 25%.
  • Among suppliers, Food from Da Yard and La Bella Italia generate the most net revenue. Fromage pour Tous earns only 16% on 514K in gross revenue.

The product and supplier charts exclude Easter Bun, so their bars add up to slightly less than the cards.

How it's calculated

Gross Revenue = Orders[Quantity] * RELATED(Products[ListPrice])
Cost = Orders[Quantity] * RELATED(Products[UnitCost])
Net Revenue = Orders[Gross Revenue] - Orders[Cost]
Net Margin % = DIVIDE(SUM(Orders[Net Revenue]), SUM(Orders[Gross Revenue]))

Data model

The data model as it appears in Power BI: five tables connected by four relationships (1 = one, * = many). Click to view full size.

How the tables connect

  • Customers → Orders: CustID matches Cust ID
  • Products → Orders: ProdID matches Product ID
  • Suppliers → Products: SupplierID matches SupID
  • Countries → Suppliers: CountryName matches Country

Each link is one-to-many: one customer, product, supplier, or country connects to many rows on the other side.
