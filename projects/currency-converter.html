Why I built it

I wanted to know what things really cost abroad, so I built a currency converter for my own use. Then I shared it with my family and the people close to me, and they've been using it when they travel abroad. I gave my idea to AI, and it helped me turn what I wish I had into a real app that I use.

How it helps you

  • Quick: Type a price, pick two currencies, and see the result right away.
  • Shop with confidence: Know what something really costs before you pay, so you can decide better when you shop abroad.
  • Made for travelers: It covers eight currencies, with nothing extra to scroll through.
  • Always with you: It lives on your home screen, one tap away.

How I built it

I gave AI my idea and the list of currencies I needed, and it helped me build the app. Here's what's inside:

  • The screen: One simple page with an amount box, two currency pickers, a swap button, and the result.
  • The rates: When you convert, the app asks the ExchangeRate-API for the latest rate, and it saves each rate on your device for an hour so repeat conversions are instant.
  • The small details: It remembers the last two currencies you picked, and it waits a moment while you type so it isn't fetching on every keystroke.
  • On your phone: It's a progressive web app, so you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
  • Online: It's hosted on Vercel, so anyone with the link can use it.

Built with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
