Net Revenue Dashboard in Tableau

Click a country, and the whole dashboard updates.

Data visualization · Tableau · Course project

Course dataset: a fictional food distributor. The screenshots show the interactive dashboard as exported from Tableau.

The question

A food distributor buys 60 products from suppliers in nine countries. Where does its net revenue come from, and which products deserve a closer look?

How it works

Clicking a country on the map filters the headline numbers, the product chart, and the drinks comparison to that country. The map's measure and the number of lowest sellers can be changed with controls. Clicking an empty part of the map clears the filter.

What I found

  • Revenue is concentrated: five of the nine supplier countries bring 79% of net revenue, and the USA alone brings 23.5%.
  • Drinks earn more per order ($199 vs. $138, or 44% more), but it depends on the drink: Coffee earns $506 per order, while the two teas from China earn $45 and $89.
  • The ten lowest sellers bring only 1.7% of gross revenue, so they're worth a review. The data has no costs for stocking a product, so it can't say whether to drop them.
